# virgoAi pricing and credits

This is the rate card the app charges from. The tables below are generated by `php artisan billing:rate-card`
from the live price tables, the rate table in `config/pricing.php`, and the owner's margin. Re-run the command after
any price or margin change. Run `php artisan billing:rate-card --check` in continuous integration: it fails if any
price sells below its cost plus the margin.

## How a price is made

1. **Cost.** Each provider call has a cost: Claude by tokens (per million), fal by the image or the second of video
   the call is billed for. fal's cost comes from `config/pricing.php` (`fal_rates`), read from fal's model pages,
   or from the synced fal price for models without a rate table.
2. **Sell price.** Cost plus the margin (40% by default, never below the 40% floor), rounded up to the micro-dollar.
3. **Credits.** One credit is $0.001 of sell price, so $1 is 1,000 credits. Usage is billed in credits.
4. **Director package.** A film is priced in three parts: the director (the story bible and one call per chapter),
   the board (one reference per cast member and location, at most 11), and the takes (each one at its exact length).
   Each part is priced the same way, so the package is the sum of its parts.

## What is charged, and when

- **Reserved up front.** The whole estimate is reserved when a film or a full video starts. An estimate that is not
  enough makes the start fail, before anything is sent.
- **Director calls** are charged at the tokens actually used, as soon as the reply arrives, including a reply that is
  thrown away.
- **Board references and takes** are charged at their quoted price when they are sent to fal. The price is read again
  when a take is sent. If it has risen, the film is checked against its reservation again before the take goes out.
- **Failed jobs** are not charged. This assumes fal does not bill a job that fails. Confirm this on a live account.
- **Stopped films** charge the work already sent, because fal bills it, and return the rest of the reservation.
- **Settlement** can never charge more than was reserved. If a provider cost more than its reservation, the overrun
  is logged as an error for the owner, not charged to the user silently.

## Where the money is not kept

- The "kept after fees" column subtracts a percentage payment fee from the package price (5%, placeholder in
  `config/pricing.php`). The per-purchase fixed fee is not spread across packages, so the figure is optimistic for
  small top-ups until the processor's real rates are entered.
- The plan image figure uses the dearest image tier. The charge uses the size and quality actually sent, so a cheaper image costs the user less.

## Plan entitlements

Plans promise the dearest image (the highest tier, sold at the margin) and the 720p second of video, so a plan never
gives more than the cost allows.
