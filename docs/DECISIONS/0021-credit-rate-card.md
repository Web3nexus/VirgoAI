# 0021 — Credit Rate Card and Margin Floor

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Decision record:** DEC-0021 in [`../DECISION_REGISTER.md`](../DECISION_REGISTER.md)
- **Rate card:** [`../PRICING.md`](../PRICING.md), generated by `php artisan billing:rate-card`

## Context

A user's charge is built from the provider calls behind it: Claude for the director, GPT Image for the board and
keyframes, and a video model for each take. The stored fal price is one number per model, so it could not price
size, quality or resolution. A check against fal's model pages found the app charging too little for several calls:

- GPT Image at landscape or vertical size costs $0.050 to $0.051 at medium quality. The stored price was $0.034, the
  square size.
- Seedance at 1080p costs $0.682 a second. The stored price was the 720p rate, so a 1080p take was about 55% under cost.
- Veo bills 4, 6 or 8 seconds and Hailuo 6 or 10, but a 5-second request was quoted as 5.
- The margin could be set to 0%, and payment fees would then turn every sale into a loss.

## Decision

1. **Rate table in config.** `config/pricing.php` (`fal_rates`) prices each fal call by what is sent: image size
   and quality, video resolution, and the seconds the provider bills. A call outside the table is refused, not guessed.
2. **Billed seconds.** `FalEndpoints::billableSeconds` follows each payload builder, so quotes match what fal bills.
3. **Margin floor.** The margin cannot go below 40%, in the admin endpoint or in the value read back.
4. **Credits.** One credit is $0.001 of sell price. Sell price is cost plus margin, rounded up.
5. **Director package.** A film is priced as its parts: the director, the board, and the takes. Each part is sold
   at its own rounded-up price.
6. **Safety on the money path.**
   - Director calls are recorded when they arrive, including retries and replies that are thrown away.
   - A take's price is read again when it is sent. A rise is checked against the reservation before it goes out.
   - Production scripts are recorded before they are parsed.
   - A failed production or film settles to the work that was sent, not to a full refund.
7. **Verification.** `billing:rate-card --check` fails when a price sells below cost plus margin. It is meant to run
   in continuous integration.

## Consequences

- Prices now follow the call. Cheap calls cost less for the user, and the high-resolution and high-quality calls
  that were under-priced now cost what they cost us.
- Several rates are not verified (see the table in `PRICING.md`): the Seedance reference endpoint, the GPT Image edit
  endpoint, and Hailuo 512P. Each one needs a live call or a page read before the price is relied on.
- The one-hour film at 720p reserves about $1,531. The default $500 per-film cap refuses it. Raising the cap is a
  financial decision for the owner.
- Payment fees are a placeholder (5% and $0.50). The rate card's "kept after fees" figure is only as good as that input.
